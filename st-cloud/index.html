Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in St. Cloud

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

Small problems don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
